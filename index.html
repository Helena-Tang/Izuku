Izuku Midoriya

About Izuku Midoriya

Izuku Midoriya, often known by his hero name Deku, is the determined, compassionate, and highly intelligent main protagonist of the widely popular anime and manga series My Hero Academia, created by Kohei Horikoshi, and his story follows his inspiring journey from a powerless boy born without a Quirk in a superpowered society to a courageous young hero chosen to inherit the legendary power known as One For All, pushing himself beyond his limits to protect others and achieve his lifelong dream of becoming the world’s greatest hero.

What He Can Do

Izuku Midoriya can use his skills and the power of One For All to perform incredible feats such as delivering devastating super-strength punches and kicks, moving at extremely high speeds, leaping across buildings, sensing nearby danger before attacks happen, using Blackwhip to grab enemies or swing through the air, floating in midair during combat, and combining his intelligence with quick battle analysis to predict opponents’ movements and create strategies even under pressure.
